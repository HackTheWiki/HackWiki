About Hack Club

A quick history and what the community is about.

the short history

Hack Club started as a bunch of teens who wanted to build real stuff together, not just follow tutorials. what began as a few student-led clubs turned into a global community that ships projects, hosts hackathons, and runs wild experiments like hardware grants.

what actually happens here

  • Hack Clubbers build projects in their own style, then share them with the community
  • learning is hands-on. you make a thing, you learn the thing
  • if you ship, the community backs you. that is the whole point

a few vibes in one place

why the wiki exists

people keep reinventing the same wheels. this wiki is where we drop the best notes, guides, and lessons so the next Hack Clubber can pick them up fast.

if you have a build story, a trick that saved you, or a guide you wish existed, add it here.
